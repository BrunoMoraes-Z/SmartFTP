Select files and press → / ←, double-click, or drag between the panes. The queue shows progress and speed.

Agents call 17 tools: connect, upload, read_file and more. Their sessions and transfers appear live in your window.

You stay in control

Bearer token, loopback only, per-tool switches, per-site agent access. Passwords never reach the agent.
